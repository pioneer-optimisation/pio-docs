title: "Going live"
description: "How PIO picks a model's first share of users, what happens next, and what stops it doing harm."
---

A new model reaches its first users from its go-live page. Open it with Go live on the model's row on the Models page. Every value on the page starts as a safe default you can change, and one button starts the experiment.

The go-live page is the only way to give a model its first users. That includes a challenger, which starts at 0% of traffic. Once a model has served users, you change its share from the Models page.

## How the share is chosen

The share is the percentage of new users who get the model. Everyone else stays on the models already live, in the same proportions as before.

A new model has to learn before there is a change to measure. PIO counts both steps. It suggests the smallest share that should show a 5 point change in day 1 retention within about 4 weeks, and never a share so small that the model needs more than 2 weeks to learn. The suggestion never goes below 5% or above 50%.

- A larger share gives an answer sooner.
- A smaller share means fewer users see something new while it is being tested.

The suggestion rests on your game's own traffic: new users a day and how many return on day 1. Until PIO has 3 days of that traffic, it suggests 10%.

Below about 1,500 new users a day, learning alone needs an even split, so PIO suggests 50% and says how many weeks a clear answer takes. At very low traffic, even 50% can take more than 90 days to learn and show a 5 point change. The page says so. In that case the risk is a test that never finishes, not the number of users who see the model.

Move the slider or type a number between 5 and 50. The split bar and the timeline update as you do.

## What happens next

The timeline on the page counts days from the moment you start the experiment.

- Today: the model goes live for its share of new users, and measurement starts.
- Day 2: the first users reach day 1, so the first retention results arrive.
- First answer: the day a 5 point change should be visible at the chosen share, counting the time the model needs to learn. It appears once PIO can project it for your traffic.
- Day 90: the furthest PIO projects. A change still unresolved by then is too small to measure at your traffic.

## What the safety checks do

Each live model is compared with the baseline on checks that must not get worse, alongside the result it is trying to improve.

| Check | Must not fall by more than |
| --- | --- |
| Still active after 7 days | 3 percentage points |
| Users who saw no crash | 2 percentage points |

A check reads Harm when a model is confidently worse than the baseline by more than its margin. Before a model has users, its checks read Armed: they will run as soon as users arrive.

Each check is judged on its own, and there is no combined score. See [Model lifecycle](/dashboard/model-lifecycle) for where results appear after launch.

## Automatic rollback

The box "Switch it off for me if a check finds real harm" is the same setting as Automatic rollback on the Models page. It covers every model in the project.

- Off: PIO tells you when a check finds harm. The traffic stays yours to change.
- On: a model a check finds harmful has its share set to 0% without waiting for you.

Rollback acts only on stronger evidence than the Harm verdict, so a check can read Harm while the model keeps running. Putting a model back is always a manual step, and measurement restarts from that point.

Rollback is off by default while PIO gathers evidence on real traffic that its bar is set correctly.

## Per option limits

Each option in a decision can carry a cap: the largest share of that decision's users it may receive. Caps are set on the Actions page.

The go-live page lists every cap on the model's decisions. It opens the list with a warning when a cap is below an even split of that decision's options. A cap below an even split limits the option from the first user onwards.

You can set any model's share to 0% at any time from the Models page. The change reaches your game within seconds.
