---
title: "What is PIO"
description: "Player-intelligence optimisation for Unity games: the right action for each player at the decision points you define."
---

PIO picks an action for each player at decision points you define, then learns from the valued events your game already sends. Tutorial pacing, first-level difficulty, offers: you define the decision points and the candidate actions, and PIO carries the learning loop.

Your game asks for a decision, applies the returned action, and keeps playing. The events you track record how that choice worked out, and the reward is *derived* from them. Models start from a safe baseline and take traffic only as they prove themselves.
